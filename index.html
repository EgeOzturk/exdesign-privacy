Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

In short: Bouncing Ball: Moonlit Run does not collect, share, or sell your personal data. There are no ads, accounts, analytics, or tracking.

1. Who we are

This game is developed by Ex Design ("we", "us"). For privacy questions, contact: exdesign.studio@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

The game does not collect any personal data. It does not access your name, email, location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera, and it does not collect device identifiers (including advertising IDs).

3. Information stored on your device

To keep your game experience, the following settings are stored only locally on your device. They are never sent to us or to any third party:

  • Your best score
  • The ball you selected
  • Your music and sound-effects on/off preferences

This information is removed when you uninstall the app.

4. Ads, analytics, and third-party services

The game contains no ads, analytics, online accounts, in-app purchases, or social sharing. The game is built with the Unity game engine, which may process limited technical information required for the game to run. For details, see the Unity Privacy Policy.

The app stores you download from (Apple App Store, Google Play) may process certain data under their own privacy policies, which are outside our control.

5. Children's privacy

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since we do not collect personal information from any user, no children's data is held.

6. Data security and your rights

Because we do not process personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete under laws such as GDPR, CCPA, or Turkey's KVKK. You are still welcome to contact us with any questions.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available on this page with an updated effective date. If we add features such as ads or analytics in the future, we will update this policy beforehand.
